👣 The Digital Footprint Quiz

Your footprint is everything findable about you: posts, tags, public profiles, forgotten accounts. Answer 10 honest questions — there are no wrong answers, only useful ones.

🧹 Shrink it: the universal reduction plan

🔒 Lock the profilesSet accounts to friends-only, hide your friends list and date of birth, turn on tag review. Thirty minutes, biggest single win.
🗑 Delete the dead accountsOld forums, games and quiz apps keep your data on forgotten, often unpatched servers. Delete — don't just abandon.
📍 Post later, not liveShare the holiday when you're home. Real-time location posts advertise an empty house and a daily routine.
🔎 Search yourself quarterlyName + city, name + phone, your email in a breach checker. You can't fix what you haven't seen.
